Block and inline formatting

Paragraphs are the bread and butter of block layout. This one has bold, italic, bold italic, inline code, a link to a fragment, a span with a background and some text that is long enough to wrap onto several lines at a 1280 pixel viewport when the page is capped at fifty-six rems wide. Wrapping happens at UAX #14 break opportunities, hyphens-included, and the hanging space at the end of a line must not push text past the edge.
